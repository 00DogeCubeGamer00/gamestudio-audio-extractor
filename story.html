Creator's Note

The Love for Dejobaan Games & Why I Built This

Turning years of soundtrack frustration into a simple, open-source tool for the gaming community.

Falling in Love with Dejobaan's Worlds

Ever since first coming across Dejobaan Games—from the chaotic, joyful destruction of The Wonderful End of the World to the high-flying adrenaline of AaaaaAAaaaAAAaaAAAAaAAAAA!!! — A Reckless Disregard for Gravity—their creative energy and unforgettable soundtracks immediately stood out.

Indie games from that era have a distinct soul, and their music is a huge part of what makes them stick with you years later. Naturally, I always wanted to pull those audio tracks out of the game files so I could listen to them cleanly, study how they were put together, and appreciate the sound design up close.

The Frustration with Legacy A4–A6 Tooling

When I went looking for a way to extract the music, I hit a wall. Despite how many classic indie titles were built on Conitec's 3D Gamestudio (A4, A5, and A6) engines, there was a glaring lack of tools that were actually easy and simple to use.

"I always wanted to extract the audio within their games, but felt frustrated that nobody had made tools for A6/A4 that were simple, straightforward, and easy to use."

Existing unpackers were either outdated, overly complicated, broken on modern operating systems, or unable to handle the bitwise obfuscation hiding the .ogg and .wav streams inside audio_resource.dat. Instead of giving up on getting those tracks, I decided to sit down, dig into the file structure myself, and compile a clean solution from scratch.

Giving Back to the Gaming Scene

What started as a personal mission to rescue the music from my favorite Dejobaan titles turned into something much bigger: a lightweight, universal extractor that anyone can run in seconds without needing deep reverse-engineering knowledge.

For the Community: I put this together and shared it publicly in the hope that it helps fellow soundtrack lovers, modders, archivers, and anyone else in the gaming scene who has ever wanted an effortless way to unlock and preserve audio from classic 3D Gamestudio games.
Made out of appreciation for classic indie games and the community that keeps them alive.
